1. Who we are

Designers, engineers and marketers at one table — so nothing gets lost between the idea and the launch.

We started Halflight in 2016 because we were tired of beautiful designs arriving broken in the browser, and of brands launched with nobody to help them grow.

Today we are fourteen people in a first-floor studio on Rua das Flores, with a desk in London. We take on around six projects a year, and most clients stay with us long after launch.

3. How we work

No black boxes. Real, clickable progress every Friday.

  1. Week 1 — 201

    Discover

    Workshops, audits and research. We learn your business before we draw a single box.

  2. Week 2 — 302

    Define

    Strategy, sitemap and one clear creative idea, agreed in writing.

  3. Week 3 — 1003

    Design & build

    Design and engineering in parallel weekly sprints — prototypes, not slides.

  4. Ongoing04

    Launch & grow

    Launch, then campaigns, SEO and iteration. We stay on as part of your team.
